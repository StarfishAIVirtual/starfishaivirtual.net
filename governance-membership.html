Questions

Frequently asked

Why a membership instead of a consulting retainer?

A retainer pays for hours, so the incentive is more hours. A membership pays for access, so our incentive is to make the kits work quickly and well enough that you renew.

Can I buy a kit without a membership?

No. Like a warehouse club, the catalog is members only. That's what lets us keep kit prices close to cost.

How does the 10% reward work?

Command Members earn 10% of what they spend on kits during the membership year as credit, applied to the next year's membership or kits.

Is this compliance software?

No. Starfish focuses on decision authority and human accountability: who decides, who can stop a decision, and who answers for it. It works alongside whatever compliance tools you already use.
