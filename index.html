Our Frameworks

Three gates between AI and your reputation

Proprietary Starfish frameworks built on four decades of systems and performance engineering.

01

Ethical Gate

A defined checkpoint every AI-assisted decision passes before it reaches a customer or the public, with a named person who can stop it.

02

Human-In-Command

AI recommends; people decide. Clear lines of authority so the final call, and the accountability, always sits with a human.

03

Iteration Cap

A limit on how many times AI output is retried before a person steps in, so errors don't quietly compound.

We focus on decision authority and human accountability, not checkbox compliance tooling.
