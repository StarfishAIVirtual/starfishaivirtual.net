Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short

  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used.
  • Your individual Diagnostic answers never leave your browser. Only your overall score and stage are counted.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • You can turn analytics off at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

This policy explains how Starfish Enterprises International, doing business as Starfish AI Virtual ("Starfish", "we", "us"), of San Jose, California, handles information when you visit starfishaivirtual.net (the "site").

What we collect

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4, a service from Google LLC. When analytics is on (see "Your choices"), it sets cookies and collects information such as:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay
  • your approximate location (country and city)
  • your device, browser and the site that referred you
  • these actions on the site:
    • starting the Governance Diagnostic from one of its buttons
    • completing the Diagnostic, recorded once per visit with your overall score and stage only
    • clicking our phone, email or LinkedIn links, or the "join" and membership links
    • opening one of our free white papers, recorded with which paper you opened
    • using the cost calculator, recorded when you leave or switch away from the page (again only if you change your choices), with only the number of AI systems and the number of kits you selected

Each tracked click is recorded with which link was clicked and the page it was on. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. We don't send your name, email address or individual Diagnostic answers to Google Analytics. Google Signals and ad personalization are switched off in our analytics code, so this data is not used for advertising.

Diagnostic and calculator. Your answers to each Diagnostic question, and the kits you pick in the cost calculator, are worked out in your browser. They are not sent to us or stored.

When you contact us. If you email or call us, we receive what you choose to share: typically your name, email address, phone number, company and your message. We use it to reply to you and, if you become a member, to provide our services.

Fonts. The site's fonts are hosted on this site, so loading them sends nothing to a third party.

How we use it

  • to understand how the site is used and improve it
  • to respond to your enquiries and provide services you ask for
  • to keep records required by law

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to make automated decisions about you.

Who receives it

Google receives the analytics information described above, only when analytics is on, as our service provider, under its own terms and privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We may also disclose information if required by law. We don't share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, after which Google deletes it. We keep emails and contact details for as long as we have a working relationship with you, or as long as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Visitors in Europe (based on your device's time zone), and visitors whose device reports no local time zone, are asked first. Analytics only starts if you click Accept.
  • Visitors elsewhere have analytics on by default. Click Cookie settings at the bottom of any page and choose Decline to turn it off; we also remove our analytics cookies from your browser. Your choice is remembered on that browser.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this signal, analytics never starts.
  • You can also install Google's Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or block cookies in your browser. The site works fully without them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California under the CCPA/CPRA, and the EU or UK under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you
  • get a copy of it
  • have it corrected or deleted
  • object to or limit how we use it

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Children

The site is meant for business leaders. It is not directed to children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
